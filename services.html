FAQ

Your questions, answered

In Imani's own words, from the FAQ they share with couples.

What services do you offer?

At Imani Events, we offer full wedding planning, event coordination, destination wedding planning, décor styling and design, vendor management, bridal assistance, and traditional wedding planning services tailored to your vision and needs.

Do you only plan weddings?

While weddings are our specialty, we also plan and coordinate luxury celebrations, private events, bridal showers, engagement parties, corporate events, and intimate gatherings.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking at least 6–12 months in advance for weddings to allow ample time for planning, vendor sourcing, and securing your preferred dates. However, we also accommodate shorter timelines depending on availability.

Do you offer on-the-day coordination?

Yes. Our coordination service ensures that your wedding or event runs smoothly while you remain fully present in the moment. We manage timelines, vendors, setup, and guest flow on your behalf.

Can you plan intimate weddings and private celebrations?

Absolutely. Whether it's an intimate gathering or a grand celebration, we create experiences that feel personal, elegant, and unforgettable.

What happens during the consultation?

Our consultation is an opportunity to understand your vision, style, priorities, and expectations. We'll also discuss budget guidance, timelines, and the services best suited for your event.

Do you offer destination wedding planning?

Yes. We curate and coordinate destination weddings both within Kenya and beyond, ensuring a seamless experience for you and your guests from start to finish.

Can you work within our budget?

Absolutely. We believe luxury is about experience and intentional design. We work closely with our clients to maximize their budget while maintaining elegance, quality, and attention to detail.

Do you provide décor and styling services only?

Yes. If you already have a planner or venue but need professional styling and décor execution, we offer standalone décor design and styling services.

Will you help us find vendors?

Yes. We connect our clients with trusted vendors including photographers, caterers, florists, entertainers, makeup artists, cake designers, and more to ensure a cohesive and elevated event experience.

Do you handle traditional weddings?

Yes. We beautifully plan and coordinate traditional ceremonies while honoring cultural traditions and incorporating modern sophistication where desired.

How involved will we need to be in the planning process?

Your level of involvement is entirely up to you. Some clients prefer to be hands-on, while others trust us to manage every detail. We tailor the experience to suit your preferences and lifestyle.

Do you travel for events?

Yes. We are available for events across Kenya and selected international destinations.

What makes Imani Events different?

We focus on creating deeply intentional and visually stunning celebrations. Our approach combines refined aesthetics, thoughtful planning, personalized service, and seamless execution to deliver unforgettable experiences.

How do we get started?

You can begin by reaching out through WhatsApp, email, or social media to schedule a consultation. We'll discuss your vision, requirements, and how we can bring your celebration to life.
