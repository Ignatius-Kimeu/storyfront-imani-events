Our profile

“I'm Eunah, lead planner and founder.”

Imani Events is a Nairobi-based luxury wedding and event planning brand dedicated to creating timeless, beautifully curated celebrations across Kenya.

With a refined eye for detail and a passion for intentional design, we specialise in bespoke planning, elegant décor styling and seamless event execution tailored to each client's unique vision.

From intimate traditional ceremonies to sophisticated destination weddings, we transform meaningful moments into unforgettable experiences defined by beauty, grace and excellence.
